# From the windowed bound (⋆) to GRH: the analytic part of the announced proof, in human-readable form

*Vincent P. P. NOËL, MSc Cogn. Neurosci. (independent, amateur) — written with Claude (Anthropic) — 23 November 2026, fourth version, revised after three independent checks. Unofficial reading aid. Lean source consulted: `leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis`, commit `51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d`. Declaration names are given in brackets, with a link to their line at that commit. Errors are ours; please report them.*

## 0. Scope

The announced proof has one hard arithmetic estimate, called (⋆) in the [architecture guide](https://nasqret.github.io/riemann-rev/guide/proof-guide.pdf): for each primitive character ψ, a weighted ramp statistic of the characters generated by ψ is at most x^(½ + 9ε) on windows [Y, Y^(3/2)] with Y arbitrarily large. The guide proves it in §§4–7. This note does **not** treat (⋆). It gives a complete human-readable proof of everything **after** it:

  (⋆) for every primitive ψ  ⟹  no zero of any Dirichlet L-function with Re s > ½  ⟹  zeros in the strip lie on Re s = ½  ⟹  RH.

In Lean, (⋆) is not a single hypothesis. It is assembled from several separate inputs (§5 below lists them). We keep two things apart:
- **the analytic deduction**, §§1–4 and 6, which we prove in full;
- **the formal inputs that produce (⋆)**, §5, which we name, define and locate, but whose proofs we do not reproduce. One of them, the averaging lemma behind `level_gm_le_on_family_scales`, is the guide's §5 and belongs to the arithmetic side.

The core analytic lemma (the windowed Landau theorem) is written out for ζ in a companion note (`TAC-017/PREUVE.md`, in French). §2 below is self-contained and covers families. The proofs of the lemmas cited were read in the Lean source, except where marked *(statement only)*.

## 1. Objects

**Primitive characters** [[`Formalism.HC.PChar`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/PrimePairSizeCondition.lean#L11)]. A PChar is a primitive Dirichlet character ψ of conductor N. `PChar.one` is the trivial character mod 1, whose L-function is ζ. `PChar.of χ` is the primitive character inducing χ.

**Levels** [[`Formalism.HC.Level`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/ExplicitFormulaErrorOfHostConditions.lean#L12), [`KSet`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/PrimePairSizeCondition.lean#L37)]. A level Λ consists of:
- a finite set KEPT of PChars, containing `one` and closed under ψ ↦ ψ⁻¹ ;
- an optional distinguished real odd character `psK` ;
- an integer o.

Its weights are wt(ψ) = 1 if ψ = psK, and (3o + 1)⁻¹ otherwise [[`Level.wt`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/ExplicitFormulaErrorOfHostConditions.lean#L36)]. Its statistic is [[`Level.Gm`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/ExplicitFormulaErrorOfHostConditions.lean#L38)]

  Gm(y) = G(y) + Σ_{ψ ∈ KEPT, ψ ≠ 1} wt(ψ) · Re g_ψ(y),

with the centred ramp sums of guide §2 [[`Deduction.G`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/CharFamily.lean#L25), [`Deduction.Gchi`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/CharFamily.lean#L19)]:
- G(y) = Σ_{n≤y} Λ(n)(y/n − 1) − y(log y − 1 − γ) ;
- g_ψ(y) = Σ_{n≤y} Λ(n)ψ(n)(y/n − 1) + y·L′/L(1, ψ) for ψ ≠ 1.

**The cyclic level of ψ** [[`Level.cyc`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/ExplicitFormulaErrorOfHostConditions.lean#L118), [`Level.ofSub`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/ExplicitFormulaErrorOfHostConditions.lean#L102)]. KEPT is the set of primitive characters inducing the powers of ψ, o is the order of ψ, and there is no psK. Its weights are therefore 1 on the trivial character and 1/(3o + 1) on the others. These are the positive weights P(ψ) of guide §3. Moreover ψ ∈ KEPT [[`Level.mem_cyc`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/ExplicitFormulaErrorOfHostConditions.lean#L120)].

## 2. Theorem 1 — windowed Landau for a positive family

**Statement** [[`Deduction.zeroFree_of_rpow_window`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/ZeroFreeOfRpowWindow.lean#L15)]. Let F be a finite family of characters χ_i with weights a_i > 0, where each χ_i is either the trivial character mod 1 or non-trivial [[`Deduction.CharFamily`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/CharFamily.lean#L28), field `adm`]. Let G_F(y) = Σ a_i Re g_{χ_i}(y) [[`Deduction.Gm`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/CharFamily.lean#L40)]. Let 0 < θ < 1, δ > 0 and C be real. Suppose that for every Y₀ there is Y ≥ Y₀ with G_F(y) ≤ C y^θ for all y ∈ [Y, Y^(1+δ)]. Then no L(s, χ_i) vanishes for Re s > θ.

**Proof.** We work in the variable u = log y and write g(u) = G_F(eᵘ).

*Step 1: the transform and its poles.* Let Φ_F(s) = ∫₀^∞ g(u) e^(−su) du, defined for Re s > 2 because |g(u)| ≤ C₀ e^(2u) [[`Gchi_growth`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/GchiGrowth.lean#L41)]. Since Re g_χ = (g_χ + g_χ̄)/2, [[`poleData`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/poleData.lean#L252)]

  Φ_F(s) = Σ_i (a_i/2) · ( Φ_{χ_i}(s) + Φ_{χ_i⁻¹}(s) ),

where Φ_χ is the transform of g_χ [[`poleData_char`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/poleData.lean#L141)]. Φ_χ continues meromorphically to Re s > 0 and has a simple pole at each zero ρ of L(s, χ), with residue m_χ(ρ) · (−1/(ρ(ρ − 1))), where m_χ is the multiplicity. Hence the poles of Φ_F in Re s > 0 are exactly the zeros of the L(s, χ_i) and of their conjugates [[`zeroSet`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauZeroFreeCriterion.lean#L15)], with residue

  c(ρ) = −(1/(ρ(ρ − 1))) · Σ_i (a_i/2) (m_{χ_i}(ρ) + m_{χ_i⁻¹}(ρ)).

The sum is a positive combination of non-negative integers, at least one of which is positive. So **c(ρ) ≠ 0: zeros of different members cannot cancel** (guide §3, idea 1). If ρ is real, ρ(ρ − 1) < 0 and c(ρ) > 0. From here on the proof uses only these properties, packaged as [`PoleData`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauZeroFreeCriterion.lean#L19): growth |g(u)| ≤ C₀e^(2u), the Laplace representation for Re s > 2, meromorphy on Re s > 0 with isolated poles, simple poles with non-zero residues, positive residues at real poles. The abstract lemma is [[`Deduction.landau_window`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/landauWindow.lean#L87)].

*Step 2: the window in the variable u.* Put U = log Y. The hypothesis gives, on [U, (1 + δ)U], the bound g(u) ≤ R(u) with R(u) = |C| e^(θu) ≥ 0, and

  ∫_U^{(1+δ)U} R(u) e^(−θu) du = |C| δ U ≤ e^(ηU)

for every fixed η > 0 once U is large [[`windowHyp_of_rpow`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/WindowHypOfRpow.lean#L31), [`WindowHypU`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauZeroFreeCriterion.lean#L29)]. So, for every η > 0, there are arbitrarily large U with this property.

*Step 3: the moments.* For Re s > 2 and an integer k ≥ 0, put

  A_k(s) = (−1)^k Φ_F^(k)(s) / k! = ∫₀^∞ g(u) w_k(u) e^(−i(Im s)u) du,  where w_k(u) = u^k e^(−(Re s)u) / k! ≥ 0

[[`landau_coeff`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/LandauCoeff.lean#L14)]. These are the Taylor coefficients of Φ_F at s, up to the sign (−1)^k. Near a simple pole ρ with residue c, A_k(s) = c/(s − ρ)^(k+1) + (Taylor coefficient of the regular part).

*Step 4: the one-sided inequality.* Fix a real σ > 2 and γ ∈ ℝ. Since g is real, |g| + g = 2g⁺, where g⁺ = max(g, 0). Therefore

  |A_k(σ + iγ)| + Re A_k(σ) ≤ ∫₀^∞ (|g| + g) w_k = 2 ∫₀^∞ g⁺ w_k,

with w_k taken at Re s = σ. This is the only place where "one side suffices" enters, and it uses only that g is real. We split the last integral [[`landau_window_ineq`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/landauWindowIneq.lean#L31)]:
- *Inside the window* [U, (1 + δ)U], g⁺ ≤ R. Since u^k e^(−(σ−θ)u)/k! ≤ (σ − θ)^(−k) for u ≥ 0 [[`landau_kernel_max`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/LandauKernelMax.lean#L13)], we have w_k(u) ≤ e^(−θu)/(σ − θ)^k. The window contributes at most 2e^(ηU)/(σ − θ)^k.
- *Outside the window*, g⁺ ≤ |g| ≤ C₀e^(2u), so the contribution is at most 2C₀ ∫_{outside} u^k e^((2−σ)u)/k! du. For σ large in terms of δ, the tail lemma gives, for every U ≥ 1, an integer k ≥ U with this tail at most C′/σ^k. *(Statement read. The idea is that the kernel u^k e^((2−σ)u) peaks at u = k/(σ − 2), and k is chosen so that the peak falls inside the window.)* [[`landau_tails`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/landauTails.lean#L85)].

Since σ^(−k) ≤ (σ − θ)^(−k), we obtain the **key inequality** [[`landau_window_key`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/landauWindow.lean#L44)]: there are σ > 2 and C₁ such that, for every η > 0, there are arbitrarily large U and an integer k ≥ U with, uniformly in γ,

  |A_k(σ + iγ)| + Re A_k(σ) ≤ C₁ e^(ηU) / (σ − θ)^k.   (K)

*Step 5: a pole would violate (K).* Suppose some L(s, χ_i) vanishes at ρ₀ with Re ρ₀ > θ. Fix σ as in (K). For the abstract `PoleData`, every pole has real part ≤ 2 < σ [[`landau_pole_re_le`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/LandauPoleReLe.lean#L33)]. For the Dirichlet L-functions considered here, every zero in fact has Re ρ ≤ 1, but the proof needs only σ to the right of all poles. The point ρ₀ is at **horizontal** distance σ − Re ρ₀ < σ − θ from σ, while its Euclidean distance may be much larger. The dichotomy [[`landau_dichotomy`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/LandauDichotomy.lean#L28)] is: either
- (A) there is D ≤ σ − θ such that the disc B(σ, D) contains no pole but some pole lies at horizontal distance < D; or
- (B) there is a real pole β > θ which is the only pole in some disc B(σ, r) with σ − β < r ≤ σ − θ.

To see this: if the closed disc of radius σ − θ contains no pole, take D = σ − θ. Otherwise let d be the least distance from σ to a pole. If some pole has horizontal distance < d, take D = d. If not, every pole at distance d is real, and it gives (B).

The contradiction comes from one elementary fact [[`landau_growth_contra`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/LandauGrowthContra.lean#L13), [`landau_window_contra`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/landauWindow.lean#L24)]. Let 0 < d < e ≤ E, c > 0 and M, C₁ real. Take η = ½ log(e/d), which is positive and smaller than log((σ − θ)/d) when E = σ − θ. Then an inequality c/d^k ≤ M/e^k + C₁e^(ηU)/E^k cannot hold for arbitrarily large U and k ≥ U. Indeed, since U ≤ k and e ≤ E, the right-hand side is at most (|M| + |C₁|) e^(ηk)/e^k. Multiplying by d^k, the inequality would give c ≤ (|M| + |C₁|) e^(−k log(e/d)/2), and the right-hand side tends to 0. This is why k must be at least U: the loss e^(ηU) from the window is beaten by the gain (e/d)^k from the pole.

- *Case (B).* Take γ = 0 in (K) and drop |A_k(σ)| ≥ 0. Let d = σ − β, and e = (d + r)/2 < r ≤ σ − θ. The pole β is alone in B(σ, r), so the Cauchy estimates for the regular part on B(σ, e) give Re A_k(σ) ≥ c(β)/d^(k+1) − M/e^k [[`landau_pole_asym`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/LandauPoleAsym.lean#L23)]. Here c(β) > 0, which is the positivity of residues at real poles. With (K), this gives c(β)/d^(k+1) ≤ M/e^k + C₁e^(ηU)/(σ − θ)^k, which is impossible.
- *Case (A).* Move the centre vertically to z = σ + iγ so that a single pole ρ is nearest to z, at distance d, and is the only pole in some B(z, r) with d < r ≤ D [[`landau_nearest`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/LandauNearest.lean#L40)]. Let e = (d + r)/2. The pole term gives |A_k(z)| ≥ |c(ρ)|/d^(k+1) − M/e^k [[`landau_pole_asym`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/LandauPoleAsym.lean#L23)]. The real-axis term may be negative, but Φ_F is holomorphic on B(σ, D) and e < D, so Cauchy's estimates give Re A_k(σ) ≥ −M′/e^k [[`landau_cauchy`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/LandauCauchy.lean#L13)]. Summing and using (K), we get |c(ρ)|/d^(k+1) ≤ (M + M′)/e^k + C₁e^(ηU)/(σ − θ)^k, which is again impossible, because c(ρ) ≠ 0 and e < r ≤ D ≤ σ − θ.

In particular, a real zero close to 1, a "Siegel zero", needs **no separate treatment**. A real initial zero does not by itself force case (B): depending on the other poles near σ, the dichotomy may land in case (A) instead. Both branches are covered by the proof above, and the real zero contributes, through the positivity of its residue, only where case (B) uses it. ∎

## 3. Theorem 2 — every level satisfies the windowed criterion

**Statement** [[`UpperTheory.Comparison₂.formB`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/PrimitiveCosets/Imports.lean#L12); definitions [`Level.FormB`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/Window.lean#L28), [`Level.FormBAt`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/Window.lean#L25), [`HasWindows`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/Window.lean#L11)]. Let Λ be a level, λ > 1 and 0 < θ < 1. Suppose that for every y₀ there is Y ≥ y₀ such that Λ.Gm(x) ≤ x^θ for all x ∈ [Y, Y^λ]. Then every ψ ∈ KEPT has L(s, ψ) ≠ 0 for Re s > θ.

**Proof.** Form the family F indexed by KEPT, with the weight of ψ equal to 1 if ψ is trivial and Λ.wt(ψ) otherwise. All weights are positive, and every member is admissible: a primitive principal character has conductor 1. Then G_F = Λ.Gm, by unfolding the definitions. Apply Theorem 1 with δ = λ − 1 and C = 1. ∎

*Remark.* Only the positivity of the weights is used. The specific values 1 and 1/(3o + 1) play no role here: they come from the averaging of guide §5 (see §5 below).

## 4. Theorem 3 — the order of choices

**Statement** [[`Formalism.HC.level_of_tiedWin`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/TopWin.lean#L45)]. Let Λ be a level and λ > 1. Suppose there are constants e_D, e_L and ε₁ > 0 such that, for every 0 < ε < ε₁, there is a set of scales W_ε with the following two properties:
- it contains windows [Y, Y^λ] for arbitrarily large Y ;
- Λ.Gm(x) ≤ x^(½ + (e_D + e_L + 7)ε) for all large x ∈ W_ε.

Then every ψ ∈ KEPT has no zero with Re s > ½.

*(In Lean the two properties come from two separate hypotheses, see §5: the windows and the "tied" data come from `ChebyshevInequalityAtWindowScale λ Λ`, and the bound on Λ.Gm comes from `ChebyshevInequalityOnWindows Λ`. A third hypothesis, `Λ.FormBAt λ`, is Theorem 2.)*

**Proof.** Let ψ ∈ KEPT and Re s > ½. Put μ = min(Re s − ½, ½) > 0.
- Choose ε ∈ (0, ε₁) with (e_D + e_L + 7)ε ≤ μ/2 [[`exists_small_eps`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/TopWin.lean#L36)].
- Put θ = ½ + μ/2. Then ½ < θ < Re s and θ < 1.
- For large x in the windows, Λ.Gm(x) ≤ x^(½ + μ/2) = x^θ, since x ≥ 1.

So the hypothesis of Theorem 2 holds, and L(s, ψ) ≠ 0. ∎

This is the guide's "θ is chosen after the zero, and ε after θ". The guide writes the exponent as ½ + 9ε; the Lean statement carries the constants e_D + e_L + 7 explicitly. This is a difference of presentation only: the argument works for any fixed constant.

## 5. Theorem 4 — all primitive characters, and where (⋆) comes from

**Statement** [[`UpperTheory.Top.zeroFreeAll_of_topG`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ZeroFreeAllOfTopG.lean#L30)]. Let λ > 1. Assume:
- (i) [`TopG`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ZeroFreeAllOfTopG.lean#L16) ;
- (ii) for every primitive ψ, `HostExW λ (GChi ψ).req`: hosts exist on windows of parameter λ.

Then every primitive ψ has L(s, ψ) ≠ 0 for Re s > ½ [[`ZeroFreeAll`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/ExplicitFormulaErrorOfHostConditions.lean#L20)].

**The inputs, defined.**

1. *Hosts on windows* [[`HostExW`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GlobalTwisting/ThinScalesIffHostBound.lean#L102), with [`Scales`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/PrimeDiscriminants/Scales.lean#L13)]. `HostExW λ P` says: for every admissible ε (0 < ε < 1/52) there are constants and a family of scales S : `Scales λ`, with a fixed parameter S.J and a set of reals S.Win, such that

   - every scale s **belonging to S**, that is with s.eps = ε, s.J = S.J and s.x ∈ S.Win ([`Scales.Mem`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/PrimeDiscriminants/Scales.lean#L23)), and with s.x sufficiently large, carries a "host" (ranks R, a host H, an integer q₀) that satisfies the validity and quality conditions `RankVal` and `HostGood`, and the requirement P.

   For the requirement [`GChi ψ`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/Supply.lean#L46), P asks for the explicit-formula error bound `EAtH` for the cyclic level of ψ at the host, and a bound `VVle` on the lengths of certain Galois cohomology groups (Tate–Shafarevich type) of the host's number field. ## 6. Theorem 5 — imprimitive characters, the strip, and ζ

**(a) Imprimitive characters** [[`UpperTheory.Top.imprimitive`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/Imprimitive.lean#L10)]. If every primitive ψ is zero-free on Re s > ½, then so is every χ, at every s with Re s > ½ and (χ ≠ 1 or s ≠ 1). Indeed, L(s, χ) = L(s, χ*) · Π_{p | N} (1 − χ*(p) p^(−s)), where χ* = `PChar.of χ`. Each Euler factor is non-zero for Re s > 0, because |χ*(p) p^(−s)| < 1 [[`Claim.factor_ne_zero`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/HalfPlane.lean#L19)].

**(b) The strip** [[`Claim.strip`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ClaimStrip.lean#L47)]. Suppose L(s, χ) = 0 with 0 < Re s < 1.
- If Re s > ½, this contradicts (a).
- If Re s < ½, then L(s, χ*) = 0 [[`Claim.prim_zero`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ClaimStrip.lean#L11)], because the Euler factors do not vanish. The functional equation of the completed L-function of the primitive χ*, together with the non-vanishing of the gamma factor for Re > 0, gives L(1 − s, χ*⁻¹) = 0 [[`Claim.prim_reflect`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ClaimStrip.lean#L30)]. Since Re(1 − s) > ½, this contradicts (a).

Hence Re s = ½.

**(c) ζ** [[`Claim.zeta`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ClaimStrip.lean#L63), [`riemannHypothesis`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Main.lean#L13)]. Let s be a zero of ζ with s ≠ 1 and s not a trivial zero −2(n + 1).
- *s ≠ 0*, because ζ(0) = −½ (Mathlib's `riemannZeta_zero`). This must be settled first. In Mathlib, ζ(s) = Λ(s)/Γ_ℝ(s) for s ≠ 0, with Γ_ℝ(s) = π^(−s/2) Γ(s/2), and Mathlib gives Γ the value 0 at its poles. So Γ_ℝ(s) = 0 exactly for s ∈ {0, −2, −4, …}.
- *Re s > 0.* Suppose Re s ≤ 0. Since s ≠ 0 and s is not a trivial zero, Γ_ℝ(s) ≠ 0, so the completed function vanishes: Λ(s) = 0. By the functional equation Λ(1 − s) = Λ(s) = 0. Since 1 − s ≠ 0, ζ(1 − s) = Λ(1 − s)/Γ_ℝ(1 − s) = 0, with Re(1 − s) ≥ 1, which is impossible because ζ has no zero with Re ≥ 1.
- *Re s < 1*, because ζ has no zero with Re s ≥ 1. So 0 < Re s < 1, and (b) applies with the trivial character mod 1.

This is Mathlib's `RiemannHypothesis`. ∎

**(d) The formal chain to `Main.lean`, traced.** The half-plane statement in [`Main.lean`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Main.lean#L13) is [`UpperTheory.FinalTheorems.everyCharacter`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/MainConclusion.lean#L19), whose statement [`EveryCharacter`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/EveryCharacter.lean#L7) is exactly the conclusion of (a). **(e) A remark on Davenport–Heilbronn.** The Landau engine of §2 needs only `PoleData`, not an Euler product. If the Davenport–Heilbronn function, or any other "look-alike" L-function, were shown to satisfy `PoleData`, Theorem 1 would apply to it verbatim. That would give nothing, because Theorem 1 needs the window bound as a hypothesis, and **applying the engine to DH does not supply (⋆) for DH**. The bound (⋆) is produced by inputs 1–3 of §5, which use the arithmetic of characters (hosts, Galois cohomology, averaging over the characters generated by ψ). DH, which has zeros off the line, must fail there and not in §§2–4. The analytic part is therefore not a test that distinguishes true L-functions from look-alikes; the arithmetic part is.

## 7. What this part uses, and what it does not

**Uses (§§2–4, 6):**
- the Laplace transform of the ramp in the variable log y ;
- the logarithmic derivative of L near its zeros (simple poles, residue = multiplicity) ;
- a crude growth bound |g_χ(y)| ≪ y² [[`Gchi_growth`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/51d9b1a28026d458aef9c9af3df8b083276e3d2d/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/GchiGrowth.lean#L41)] ;
- the identity |g| + g = 2g⁺ for a real function ;
- Cauchy's estimates ;
- a tail bound for the kernel u^k e^(−(σ−2)u)/k! ;
- Euler factors at p | N ;
- the functional equation for primitive characters, and ζ(0) = −½.

**Does not use:**
- zero-density estimates or zero counting ;
- any bound on L′/L off the zeros ;
- any special argument for Siegel zeros ;
- the positivity of the Dirichlet coefficients: the positivity used is that of the **multiplicities of zeros** and of the **family weights** ;
- anything automorphic.

**Admitted (§5):** inputs 1–3, that is, (⋆) in its Lean form, including the character averaging `core_gm`.
